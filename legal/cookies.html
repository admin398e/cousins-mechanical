Cookie Policy

This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage used on the Cousins Mechanical Services website.

What cookies are

Cookies and browser storage are small pieces of data a website saves on your device. Some are needed for a site to work at all; others are optional and require your consent.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell what we learn to anybody.

Changing your mind

We ask once and remember your answer. If you press Reject, no analytics cookies are set. If you accepted before and want to change that, clear this site's data in your browser and we will ask again next time — we cannot remove cookies that were already placed on your device from our end.

Third-party services

Two features load from other companies when you use them: the map on the live job tracker comes from OpenStreetMap, and the anti-bot check on our sign-in forms comes from Cloudflare. Neither is used to track you across other websites. Everything we share with our suppliers is listed in our Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary storage, features such as staying signed in and tracking a live job will stop working.
