Cookie Policy

This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage used on the Cousins Mechanical Services website.

What cookies are

Cookies and browser storage are small pieces of data a website saves on your device. Some are needed for a site to work at all; others are optional and require your consent.

What we use

We keep our use of cookies to a minimum. We use only:

  • Strictly necessary storage — to keep you signed in to your account and to hold the details of a booking while you complete it. The site cannot work properly without these, so they do not require consent.
  • Security — to protect our login forms from abuse and to rate-limit repeated sign-in attempts.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

Third-party services

Some features rely on trusted third parties — for example our sign-in provider and the map used for live job tracking — which may set their own storage when you use those features. These are described in our Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary storage, features such as staying signed in and tracking a live job will stop working.
