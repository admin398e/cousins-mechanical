Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Cousins Mechanical Services Ltd collects, why, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Cousins Mechanical Services Ltd is the data controller. Company number [COMPANY NUMBER], registered office [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Contact us about your data on 01308 538046 or [privacy email address].

2. What we collect

We collect the details you give us when you book or create an account: your name, contact details (phone, email), your vehicle registration and details, the service address or location of the job, and the notes you send us about the work. When you pay, payment is handled by our card processor — we do not store full card numbers. We keep a record of the jobs you book and any account preferences.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your data only to arrange, carry out and support the jobs you book. Our lawful bases are: performance of a contract (to quote, book and complete your job); legitimate interests (service messages about a job you have booked, keeping records, preventing fraud); and consent (optional marketing messages, which you can withdraw at any time). Where the law requires it, we rely on legal obligation — for example keeping records for tax.

4. Vehicle registration lookups

When you enter a registration or tyre size we may look it up with UK Vehicle Data providers to find the correct fitment. This uses only the registration or size you provide.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with the processors that make the service work: our SMS/WhatsApp provider (job updates), Google Calendar (scheduling), our email provider (confirmations), our card payment processor, and UK Vehicle Data (registration lookups). We never sell your data. Providers act on our instructions under contract.

6. How long we keep it

We keep job records only as long as needed. Completed and cancelled jobs are automatically erased after 12 months. Records we must keep for legal or tax reasons are kept for the period the law requires and then deleted.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export or erase the data we hold, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent for marketing at any time. If you have an account you can use Download my data and Delete my account in your account area, or contact us and we will action your request. You can change marketing and text-message preferences in your account at any time.

8. Cookies

Our website uses only the storage needed to make the site work and keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
