Accessibility Statement

Cousins Mechanical Services wants everyone to be able to use our website and book our services easily.

What we do

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA standard where reasonably possible. We try to use clear language, good colour contrast, text that resizes, and a layout that works on phones, tablets and computers.

Getting help another way

If any part of the site is difficult to use, you do not need to use it to reach us. You can call 07925 340977 or 01308 538046, or message us on WhatsApp, and we will happily take your booking or answer your question directly.
