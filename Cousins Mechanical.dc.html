PRIVACY NOTICE (UK GDPR)

Cousins Mechanical Services Ltd is the data controller for the details you give us. We use your name, contact details, vehicle registration and location only to arrange, carry out and support the jobs you book — our lawful bases are performance of a contract and, for optional marketing, your consent. Service texts about a job you booked are sent under legitimate interest; marketing texts and emails only ever with your explicit opt-in, which you can withdraw any time in your account.

We share data only with the processors that make the service work — Twilio (SMS), Google Calendar (scheduling), our email provider, and UK Vehicle Data (registration lookups) — never sold to anyone. Completed and cancelled jobs are automatically erased after 12 months. You have the right to access, correct, export or erase your data: use Download my data and Delete my account in your account, or contact us.
