Cookie Policy

This page explains the cookies and similar storage used on the Cousins Mechanical Services website.

What cookies are

Cookies and browser storage are small pieces of data a website saves on your device. Some are needed for the site to work; others are optional.

What we use

We keep our use of cookies to a minimum. We use:

  • Strictly necessary storage — to keep you signed in to your account and to remember the essentials of a booking while you complete it. The site will not work properly without these, so they do not require consent.
  • Security — to protect login forms against abuse and rate-limit repeated attempts.

[If you add analytics, advertising or embedded maps/video, list them here and add a consent banner — those are not strictly necessary and require consent before they load.]

Third-party services

Some pages rely on trusted third parties (for example our booking, mapping or sign-in providers) which may set their own storage when used. These are described in our Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary storage, some features such as staying signed in may stop working.
