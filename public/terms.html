2. Quotes and bookings

A quote we give is based on the information you provide (vehicle registration, tyre size, symptoms, location). It is an estimate and not a fixed price unless we confirm it in writing. A booking is only confirmed once we send you a confirmation with a job reference. We may decline or reschedule a job if the vehicle, location or work turns out to be materially different from what was described, or is unsafe for us to work on.

3. Prices and payment

Prices for tyres and parts are shown at the time of booking and may change if supplier prices change before the job is carried out; we will tell you before fitting if a price has changed, and you are free to cancel at that point at no cost. Additional work found necessary during a job will be quoted and only carried out with your approval. Payment is due on completion unless agreed otherwise. We accept card payments on site and cash. Any discount recorded on your account is applied at our discretion and does not create an ongoing entitlement.

4. Cancellations, rescheduling and wasted call-outs

You can cancel or reschedule a booking free of charge by giving us reasonable notice — at least 24 hours where possible. If our engineer attends at the agreed time and place and cannot carry out the work because the vehicle is not available, access is not provided, or the information given was materially wrong, we may charge a call-out fee to cover our time and travel. Where such a fee applies we will have told you the amount when you booked.

5. Your responsibilities

You must give accurate details about the vehicle and the problem, make sure the vehicle is legally parked and safely accessible, and confirm that you are the owner or are otherwise authorised to instruct work on it. For roadside work you are responsible for telling us of any hazards at the location. Vehicles are worked on at your risk except to the extent that loss or damage is caused by our negligence (see section 8).

6. Tyres, parts and disposal

We fit tyres and parts suitable for your vehicle based on the size and specification confirmed at booking. Where you supply the size yourself, it is your responsibility to check it against the sidewall of your current tyre before ordering. Old tyres and parts we remove are disposed of responsibly on your behalf unless you ask to keep them; an environmental disposal charge may apply and will be shown on your quote.

7. Guarantees and workmanship

New tyres and parts carry the manufacturer’s warranty. Our own workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months or 12,000 miles, whichever comes first, against faulty fitting or installation — if something we fitted fails because of how we fitted it, we will put it right. Guarantees do not cover fair wear and tear, misuse, road-hazard damage (punctures, kerbing, impacts), damage caused by continuing to drive on a known fault, or faults in parts you supplied yourself.

Where we provide breakdown or recovery, we will use reasonable efforts to attend promptly, but arrival times are estimates and depend on traffic, weather and demand. Recovery is provided to a destination agreed with you within our operating area. For longer distances we will agree the rate with you before we set off.
